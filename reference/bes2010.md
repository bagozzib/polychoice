# Candidate-voter distance and vote choice in England, 2010 (Arzheimer and Evans 2012)

Respondents of the 2010 British Election Study face-to-face survey in
English constituencies, with the driving distance from their home to
each party's candidate, whether the candidate was the sitting MP, the
difference in local deprivation between the respondent's and the
candidate's ward, and their feelings about the parties before and after
the election (Arzheimer and Evans 2012). One row per respondent and
party (Labour, Conservative, Liberal Democrat; a party whose candidate's
distance is unknown is left out, so the choice sets vary).

## Usage

``` r
data(bes2010)
```

## Format

A data frame with 1,788 rows (674 respondents) and 9 variables:

- id:

  Respondent identifier.

- party:

  Factor: `labour`, `conservative`, `libdem`.

- chosen:

  1 for the party voted for.

- feeling:

  Feeling about the party before the election, 0 to 10.

- incumbent:

  1 if the party's candidate was the sitting MP.

- distance:

  Driving distance between the respondent's home and the candidate's.

- deprivation:

  Absolute difference in the Index of Multiple Deprivation 2010 between
  the respondent's and the candidate's ward.

- rating:

  Feeling about the party after the election, 0 to 10 (`NA` if not asked
  or not answered).

- constituency:

  Constituency name.

## Provenance and terms

From the replication archive of Arzheimer and Evans (2012) at the
Harvard Dataverse (`hdl:1902.1/17940`, version 1.0, released under CC0
1.0): the file `replication.dta` (md5 d82e986aef3f1389c6ee3babb0a15566).
The rows are those of the archive's fourth conditional logit
(`replication.do`, line 52), dropped as Stata's `clogit` drops them; the
respondents' ward identifiers and the candidates' postcodes are not
carried over. The survey is the British Election Study, 2010:
Face-to-Face Survey (Whiteley and Sanders; UK Data Service SN 7529,
[doi:10.5255/UKDA-SN-7529-1](https://doi.org/10.5255/UKDA-SN-7529-1) ),
which the UK Data Service distributes under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 licence; use of these data should cite it. The
rebuilding script is `data-raw/make_examples.R` of the development
repository.
